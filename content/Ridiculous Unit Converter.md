I have proposed a few ridiculous units of measure (see [[Fortnite]] and [[First-Down]]).
It would be great to provide a converter from conventional units to and from these.

This should be pretty straightforward to make, but it will be interesting to figure out an elegant way to incorporate location and time for the overly-literal fortnight unit.
Also, it would probably be useful to present the exact date and time at which some duration will elapse (whether that is an overly-literal fortnight or anything else).
